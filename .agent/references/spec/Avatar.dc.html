01Purpose

An avatar is the visual identity of a person or entity — a profile photo, their initials, or a fallback glyph. It anchors ownership and presence across the product: who authored a comment, who is assigned, who is online. It always degrades: image first, then color-seeded initials, then a neutral icon, so identity never renders as a broken box.

Identity
Puts a face or mark to a name across the interface.
Presence
A status ring or dot shows online, away, or busy state.
Grouping
Stacks into a group to show multiple people compactly.

10Fallbacks & seeding

The fallback chain runs image → initials → icon. Initials take the first letters of the name; their background is seeded from a hash of the name so the same person always gets the same hue, spreading people evenly across a curated palette. The final icon fallback covers unknown or system entities.
